# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want LiveQuill to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity. Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for all.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; criticize ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, harassment, discriminatory jokes or language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their consent.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and may remove comments, commits, issues or pull requests that do not follow this code. Repeated or severe violations can lead to a temporary or permanent ban from the project.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Ybucaille/livequill/security/advisories/new) (choose any title), or by contacting [@Ybucaille](https://github.com/Ybucaille) on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.
